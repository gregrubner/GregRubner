is a Los Angeles–based writer, director and motion designer who doesn’t like talking about his escape from the retiree paradise of Scottsdale, Arizona, where he was ostracized for skateboarding by mall cops and HOA representatives.

Greg brings a craft-focused approach to his maximalistic style of filmmaking, often incorporating technical tools and experimental processes in his productions. However extravagant his work tends to be, he tells funny stories about the human experience.

His surreal, absurdist comedy What the Heck Is Going On. screened at Palm Springs ShortFest and won the Industry Choice Award at Dances With Films which landed it on the 98th Oscars longlist.

Through his decade plus career in design, animation, and visual effects, Greg has contributed to projects that have garnered hundreds of millions of viewers — Super Bowl campaigns for Pepsi and Kawasaki, stage visuals for The Game Awards and SZA, music videos for Beck and Yellowcard, among many other household names.

Greg is always up to make new friends and share stories. If you’d like to get in touch or chat, he’d be more than happy to receive a hello.
